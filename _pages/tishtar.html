04 / Beyond the workbench

From movement to understanding.

The long-term vision is a robot that perceives its surroundings, localizes itself, navigates toward a destination and avoids obstacles. Each bench test is a small piece of that larger system.

Beyond the first autonomous journey lie research questions: Where should the robot look next? What does its environment mean? How might learned representations or world models support better decisions?

These are future capabilities and research directions. Autonomous navigation, semantic understanding and world-model behaviors are not implemented claims.
