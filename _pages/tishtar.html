A personal robotics project

One robot. A whole system to build.

TISHTAR is a four-wheeled mobile robot I’m building from scratch to connect perception, sensor fusion, localization and control with the challenge of autonomous navigation.

Started in October 2026, it’s my way of turning individual robotics concepts into something that can sense, move and eventually make its own way through the world.

On the workbench now

From a working gaze to ROS 2.

Camera capture, PCA9685 detection and incremental keyboard servo control have been builder-tested. The current focus is safe movement limits, documenting the setup and connecting pan-tilt control to ROS 2.

Follow the camera work ↗

Bench results are builder-confirmed. The full drivetrain and autonomous navigation are not yet verified.
